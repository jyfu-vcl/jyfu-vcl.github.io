About

I am a second-year M.Sc. student at the School of Electronic and Computer Engineering, Peking University. I am fortunate to be co-supervised by Prof. Siwei Ma (IEEE Fellow) of the Video Coding Lab and Prof. Jian Zhang of the VILLA Lab. I received my B.Eng. in 2024 from Beijing University of Technology (joint program with University College Dublin), advised by Prof. Jin Wang.

I also work in close collaboration with Prof. Yao Yao at Nanjing University.

I am currently applying for Fall 2027 Ph.D. positions. I warmly welcome potential advisors and collaborators to contact me.

Research Interests

  • 3D AIGC. Driven by the rise of generative models, I explore efficient and controllable 3D generation and editing for immersive content creation.
  • 3D Reconstruction. As immersive media emerges as a new information medium, high-quality reconstruction becomes a prerequisite for downstream applications. I focus on efficient, high-fidelity 3D reconstruction techniques for real-world scenes.
  • VLM Post-training. As vision-language models move from general-purpose understanding toward real-world applications, effectively adapting them to specialized domains becomes increasingly important. I focus on domain-specific post-training techniques for VLMs, aiming to enhance their domain knowledge, multimodal reasoning, and task adaptability.
  • Next Gen. Video Coding. Video is arguably the most important information carrier of the 21st century, and coding is the core technology that makes it deployable at industrial scale. I focus on the design of next-generation high-performance video coding frameworks, with contributions to JVET and AVS.
